Private Rental Desk · FAQ

Before you arrive.

Straight answers on approval, insurance, delivery, pricing, and the details that shape your reservation.

Need a private answer? Call the rental desk
01What do I need to rent an exotic car?

You will need a valid driver's license, proof of active auto insurance, and an approved security deposit. Vehicle-specific eligibility requirements are reviewed before your reservation is confirmed.

02How does vehicle delivery work?

Prestige Luxor is a delivery-only service with no customer-facing storefront. We confirm an eligible Southern California delivery location, arrival window, and any delivery charge with you before booking.

03Do I need my own auto insurance?

Proof of active auto insurance is reviewed later during rental approval. You do not need to provide insurance details when submitting your reservation request.

04What happens after I request a reservation?

You will see a request-received message. A team member will then contact you to confirm your car, dates, final price, and approval steps. Your reservation remains pending until we confirm it with you.

05Are the prices shown online the final rental price?

Online prices are starting daily rates. Your final rental price may change based on the vehicle, date, rental length, mileage allowance, delivery location, deposit, and selected add-ons.

06Can I add a photographer to my rental?

Yes. Select Photographer under Add-ons when requesting a reservation. We will confirm photographer availability, timing, package details, and pricing before your reservation is finalized.
