Lamborghini rental questions.

What do I need to rent a Lamborghini?+

A valid driver's license, proof of active auto insurance, rental approval, and an approved refundable security deposit. Requirements can vary by vehicle.

Do you deliver in Los Angeles and Orange County?+

Yes. Prestige Luxor is delivery-based. We confirm the eligible address, delivery window, and any applicable delivery charge with your exact quote.

Is the displayed daily price the final price?+

It is a starting daily rate. Your exact quote depends on the Lamborghini, dates, rental length, mileage, delivery location, and current availability.

Am I charged when I request availability?+

No. The form starts an availability check. Payment happens only after the rental details, approval requirements, and reservation terms are confirmed.
